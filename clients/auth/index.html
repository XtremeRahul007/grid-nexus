Terms & Privacy

This notice explains the basic rules and information handling for creating and accessing a Nexa account. Have the final legal text reviewed before using this service in production.

Terms of use

  • Provide accurate account details and keep your password confidential.
  • Use only accounts and email addresses that you are authorized to access.
  • Verification codes are temporary and must not be shared with others.
  • Do not misuse the service or attempt to access another person’s account.

Privacy

  • Your email address and username are used for account creation and sign-in.
  • Passwords are stored as password hashes; verification codes are sent to your email.
  • Authentication uses secure, HTTP-only browser cookies for verification and sessions.
  • Operational logs may include request time, IP address, browser user agent, and requested path.

These starter terms and privacy notes are not a substitute for approved legal policies.
